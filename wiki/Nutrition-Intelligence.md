# Nutrition Intelligence

Pot of Mannah is designed to answer more than “how many calories did I eat?”

The web app tracks energy, macronutrients, fiber, fats, cholesterol, minerals, and vitamins. The Nutrition section compares the day’s totals with the user’s saved daily references and ranks the largest gaps.

## Nutrition tabs

- **Overview** — daily coverage summary.
- **Missing** — nutrients with the largest remaining gaps.
- **Macros** — calories, protein, carbohydrate, fat, fiber.
- **Vitamins** — vitamin coverage.
- **Minerals** — mineral coverage.
- **Foods to Add** — foods from the local database that can help close current gaps.

## Planner

The Planner uses current nutrient gaps to surface food ideas for the next meal. Suggestions are informational and are derived from the project food database; they are not a diagnosis or medical treatment plan.
